# Interview question bank

Pick the five questions that fit the target role best. Each answer should follow STAR: situation,
task, action, result (with a number when possible), in under two minutes.

## Every role

- Tell me about yourself. (90 seconds: present, past, why this role.)
- Why do you want to leave your current job?
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager.
- What is the biggest mistake you made at work, and what did you change afterwards?
- Where do you want to be in three years?

## Engineering

- Walk me through a system you designed. What would you change today?
- Tell me about an incident you handled in production.
- How do you decide when code is good enough to ship?

## Product and design

- Tell me about a feature you killed. How did you decide?
- How do you work with engineers when the deadline cannot move?
- Show me a decision you made with incomplete data.

## Management

- Tell me about someone you had to let go or move off the team.
- How do you grow a senior person who wants to stay an individual contributor?
- How do you handle two teams competing for the same people?
