# Screening rubric (score each 1–5)

1. **Role match** – titles, scope and seniority line up with the target role.
2. **Evidence of impact** – bullets show measurable outcomes, not duties.
3. **Skills signal** – the must-have skills for the role appear in context (projects, results), not only
   in a keyword list.
4. **Clarity** – one or two pages, consistent dates, reverse chronological, scannable headings.
5. **Trajectory** – growth in responsibility; gaps or short stints are explained.

Score guide: 5 = would impress a hiring manager, 3 = acceptable, 1 = likely rejected at screening.
A total of 20+ is "Shortlist", 15–19 is "Shortlist after edits", below 15 is "Not a fit" unless the role
is junior.
