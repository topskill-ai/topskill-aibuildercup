# Clause checklist

- Parties, job title, reporting line and place of work
- Start date, and conditions precedent (work permit, background check)
- Probation period length and notice during probation
- Base salary, pay frequency, currency, salary reviews
- Bonus: target, criteria, discretion, pro-rating, clawback
- Equity: grant size, vesting schedule, cliff, leaver provisions
- Working hours, overtime, remote work policy
- Paid leave, sick leave, public holidays
- Notice period after probation (both sides), garden leave, payment in lieu
- Non-compete: scope, duration, geography, compensation during the restriction
- Non-solicitation and confidentiality
- Intellectual property assignment and prior inventions carve-out
- Relocation and visa costs, repayment obligations if the employee leaves early
- Data protection and monitoring
- Governing law, jurisdiction and dispute resolution
- Entire agreement, amendments, handbook references
