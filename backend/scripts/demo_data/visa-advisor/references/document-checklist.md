# Document checklist (core set for any work permit)

| Document | Notes | Typical lead time |
|---|---|---|
| Passport | Valid at least 12 months beyond the planned start date, two blank pages | 1–6 weeks if renewal is needed |
| Signed employment contract or binding offer | Must state job title, gross salary, hours and start date | 1 week |
| Degree certificate and transcript | Certified copy; recognition statement where the route requires it | 2–12 weeks for recognition |
| Apostille on civil and academic documents | Brazil, Mexico, India and most Hague Convention countries issue apostilles | 1–3 weeks |
| Certified translations | Into the destination language when the original is not in English | 1–2 weeks |
| Police clearance certificate | Issued less than 90 days before filing | 1–4 weeks |
| CV in the destination format | Dates, employers, responsibilities | 2 days |
| Health insurance confirmation | Valid from the first day in the country | 1 week |
| Biometric photos | Destination-specific size and background | 1 day |
| Marriage or birth certificates | Only for relocating family members; apostilled and translated | 2–4 weeks |

Tip: start the apostille and the degree recognition on day one. They are the usual longest poles.
